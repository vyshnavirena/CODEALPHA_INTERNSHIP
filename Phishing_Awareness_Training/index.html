Phishing is a common cybersecurity attack where attackers try to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

📚 What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack in which attackers use fake emails, messages, websites, or links to trick users into sharing sensitive information.

Common information attackers target:

  • Passwords
  • Banking information
  • OTP or login details
  • Personal information

📧 Phishing Examples

Example 1: Fake Email

"Your account will be suspended. Click the link immediately to verify your account."

⚠️ Warning: Urgent messages and suspicious links can be signs of phishing.

Example 2: Fake Website

A fake website may look similar to a real banking, shopping, or social media website.

⚠️ Always check the website address before entering your information.

🔐 Safety Tips

  • ✔ Check the sender's email address.
  • ✔ Don't click suspicious links.
  • ✔ Check the website URL carefully.
  • ✔ Never share passwords or OTPs.
  • ✔ Be careful with urgent messages.
  • ✔ Verify unexpected requests through official channels.
